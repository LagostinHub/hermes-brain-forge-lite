# Political Content Selection Strategy

## Overview
Strategic approach to selecting takes for political campaign videos based on emotional narrative flow and key message delivery.

## Content Structure Templates

### 1. Introduction/Appearance (0-30s)
**Key elements:** Name, age, position, energy
**Take criteria:** Positive energy, genuine smile, confident posture
**Content example:** "Olá! Eu sou [CLIENTE-B], tenho 25 anos e atualmente sou vereadora em Curitiba"

### 2. Background/Education (30s-1min)  
**Key elements:** Family background, education, motivation
**Take criteria:** Calm expression, reflective tone, clear articulation
**Content example:** "Meu amor pela educação começou dentro de casa, observando minha mãe, que é professora"

### 3. Political Journey (1min-1min30s)
**Key elements:** Early activism, key movements, personal growth
**Take criteria:** Passionate delivery, hand gestures if present, strong conviction
**Content example:** "Estou na política desde os meus 17 anos, quando comecei minha trajetória como ativista"

### 4. Current Work/Plans (1min30s-fim)
**Key elements:** Current role, key initiatives, future vision
**Take criteria:** Determined expression, forward-looking tone, authoritative presence  
**Content example:** "Hoje, com um cargo eletivo, transformo toda essa trajetória em ação"

## Selection Workflow

1. **Organize media by category** - Create folders for each section
2. **Review takes systematically** - Look for emotional match and technical quality
3. **Mark exact split points** - Use Ctrl+B for precise frame selection (API split is unreliable)
4. **Build narrative flow** - Order takes to create emotional progression
5. **Validate audio sync** - Ensure clear delivery without awkward pauses

## Technical Notes
- **Timeline naming:** Always use `_mcp_` prefix for MCP-created timelines
- **Frame accuracy:** Political content requires precise timing for message impact
- **Audio quality:** Clear voice delivery is critical for political messaging
- **Visual consistency:** Maintain consistent framing and lighting across selected takes

## Film-Decupage Integration
- Apply cinematography principles: story function first, blocking before framing
- Ensure every action has an end state (behavior, not emotion)
- Maintain continuity across selected takes (wardrobe, lighting, camera position)
- Use "Behavior, not emotion" - show political conviction through physical presence
- Match director lens to political message (documentary style for authenticity)

## Political Content Specifics
- **Candidacy videos:** Require authority and approachability balance
- **Policy explanations:** Need clear, calm delivery with visual support
- **Activism content:** Benefit from passionate delivery and hand gestures
- **Personal stories:** Work best with reflective, intimate camera angles
- **Future vision:** Require forward-looking compositions and confident energy
